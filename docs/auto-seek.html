New · a Stash plugin from Curator

Stop scrubbing. Skip to the part you want.

Auto Seek finds where the action starts in each scene, at the act you choose, and takes you there: tap Skip intro on the player, or let it skip on its own. A timeline under the player shows every act across the scene, so any other part is one tap away. Your Stash server works it all out, in the background or when you open a scene.

Install Auto Seek See how it finds the start

How it finds the start

No chapter markers needed. An image model that tells 26 acts apart reads a handful of frames and homes in, like you would with the scrubber, only faster.

Glance

It scores the 12 points Stash's own preview samples, spread across the scene, and stops as soon as the action is clearly on.

Zoom

Where the scores jump, it scans the stretch in between, frame by frame, until one is clearly past the line.

Pinpoint

A few rounds of halving the gap land it within seconds. The player jumps a moment before that, for a smoother way in.

Every act, across the whole scene

Once the start is found, Auto Seek keeps going in the background: it scans the whole scene and maps every act it sees onto a timeline under the player, live as it goes.

One lane per act

Solid blocks where each act clearly is, in the order they first show, lined up with the seek bar. A Top act strip sums it up, and the act on screen lights up.

Jump to any act

Tap an act's name to go where it first shows, and again for its next stretch. Hover a lane to see that moment's frame; tap to play from there.

Zoom into any moment

Ctrl + scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move along. Up close you get every frame's scores, a ruler, and a row of preview frames to tap.

Start where you want

Some want the kissing, some want straight to the main act. Pick a stage, or tap the acts that count for you.

Oral, hands or sex is the default; Kissing or later and Sex positions only move the line either way, and the experimental stages go back as far as a striptease. Search the 26 acts to make your own pick. Starts you marked with the flag always stay.

Two model sizes: Fast, the default, and Accurate, about four times slower per frame and much better at finding where sex positions begin.

Made for how you actually watch

One tap, or none

Skip intro sits on the player while there's an intro to skip. Turn on Skip automatically and scenes that start from the beginning jump there by themselves.

Your call wins

Disagree? Pause where it really starts and tap the flag. That scene starts there from now on, through every model update, and Undo is one tap away.

Optionally ready ahead of time

Optionally, a background task works through your library, newest first, and new scenes are picked up as a scan adds them, so starts are ready the moment you open a scene.

Stays on your server

Frames are cut by Stash's own ffmpeg and scored right there. Nothing about your library is uploaded; the model is downloaded once and checked against its checksum.

See what it saw

A small chart above the seek bar shows every frame it scored and the start it picked, so you can tell at a glance why it jumped where it did. One setting hides it.

Runs where Stash runs

Docker on x86-64 and ARM (including 32-bit), Linux, Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. Nothing extra to install: the plugin fetches what your server needs. The ARM, Windows and Mac builds are new, so tell us how they do.

Install

Three steps, then just press play

  1. 1
    In Stash, open Settings → Plugins → Available Plugins → Add Source and add this plugin source:
    https://mrx-31415.github.io/stash-plugins/main/index.yml
  2. 2
    Install Auto Seek and reload plugins. It downloads its image model (about 90 MB) and a runtime for your server (8–80 MB) in the background, once.
  3. 3
    Open any scene: Skip intro appears on the player, and the act timeline fills in below it. To have every scene ready ahead of time, run Find start points under Settings → Tasks.

Requires Stash v0.31 and the python Stash already uses for plugins. Auto Seek works on its own; you don't need Curator. Installed from Curator's source? That keeps receiving updates too. Read the guide for settings and troubleshooting.

Honest about the misses

Auto Seek is new and was tuned and tested on one library, so treat its accuracy as a first impression. There, with the default start, about 3 in 4 picks landed within a minute of a start checked by hand; for Sex positions only, 2 in 3 with the accurate model. It judges single frames, so:

  • a slow-burn opening, or people still dressed when things get going, can make it start a little late;
  • undressing or an interview without action can make it start early.

When it's wrong, the flag fixes that scene for good.

Auto Seek's models are tuned versions of LAION's CLIP ViT-B/32 and ViT-B/16, hosted at huggingface.co/mrx-31415/auto-seek, and run on ONNX Runtime. The start demo uses footage from Big Buck Bunny (CC BY 3.0, © Blender Foundation); the timeline demo is a real scene, pixelated.
